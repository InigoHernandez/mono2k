How to play

Point at a control or a line: the FM-1's own screen shows it.

Point at a control

On the drawing or in the list.

Six things to do

  1. Hear the demos

    PLAY, then PRESETS for the next demo at the bar's end.

    Space [ ]

  2. Make a beat

    GRID, tap white keys for trigs, then PLAY.

    A Z…O Space

  3. Lock a step

    Hold a step and turn a knob: that step keeps the value.

    A, hold Z + ↑

  4. Shape a sound

    SYN, ALGORITHM for the machine, the knobs for its sound.

    5 , . ↑

  5. Break the bus

    Hold FX while it plays; the black keys break the mix.

    Hold P + S…9

  6. Record a take

    PLAY, then REC: what you play lands on the nearest step.

    Space Enter

03

Sequencer

Six tracks of up to 64 steps, the Monomachine's way.

  • Locks Hold a step, turn a knob: the step keeps it.
  • Trig types A step restarts the amp, filter or LFOs alone.
  • Conditions Chance, A:B, first loop, FILL; ratchets up to four.

04

Destroy

Every track runs the Monomachine's chain; then the bus breaks it all.

  • Per track Filters, drive, EQ, rate, a mu-law delay, bits, fold, XOR.
  • The bus A 0.3–24 s reverb, glitch, tape, ADPCM, hiss, drive.
  • PERFORM Hold FX: eleven live effects on the black keys.

05

How it was made

Written from scratch, then measured against a Monomachine until the numbers agreed.

  1. The reference

    The owner's Monomachine OS, in an emulator; never in the repo.

  2. The same specs

    Every line played on both: the same notes and knobs.

  3. Measured

    Levels, spectra, times, stereo: 2,605 numbers kept.

  4. Held

    regress.py reruns every case; a known gap may only narrow.

The 90 known gaps

27 chosen
The owner's own laws: the amp's DEC, the delay send, SWAV-PUL's sweep.
36 the high-pass
The Monomachine's saturates in resonance; MONO2K's stays clean.
21 oscillators
SID's mix and top, FM feedback, ENS's chorus, a kick's onset, FX-RING.
6 corners
The EQ's top band, a rate spur, a filter envelope's start, a long ping-pong.

4 fail today: the longest delay times land up to 2 % early.

Each machine clips at its own ceiling

All once clipped at GND-SIN's: a driven SWAV-SAW bass came out 10.7 dB over the Monomachine's.

  • 24 steps, every value

    FM+'s ratio knobs decode as the Monomachine plays them: 80 of 80.

  • Four formants

    VOX-6's F1 and F2 sit within 0.2 % of the Monomachine's.

  • The owner's 64 waves

    DIGI-WAV's waves are MONO2K's and the owner's; no DigiPro copies.

  • Laws of its own

    Reverb, glitch and sends rebuilt at the owner's word; old patterns converted.

  • Bit for bit

    Integer DSP: the FM-1, this page and a computer render the same samples.

  • Clean-room

    No Elektron code, waves or samples. Not affiliated with Elektron or M-VAVE.
