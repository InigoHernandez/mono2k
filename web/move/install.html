Install

MONO2K onto the Ableton Move, through Schwung. A few minutes, over Wi-Fi.

1 · What you need

  • An Ableton Move.
  • Schwung installed on it. Schwung is unofficial, not made or supported by Ableton: back up your sets first.
  • A computer or phone on the same Wi-Fi.

2 · Install MONO2K

From the catalog. Open Schwung Manager in a browser at move.local:7700, find MONO2K and install it, once it is listed there.

As a custom module. Download the package below, then in Schwung Manager choose Install Custom Module and pick the file.

DownloadComing soon

The package isn't public yet: it comes with MONO2K for Move's first release.

3 · Open it

  1. Shift + touch the volume knob + step 13: Schwung's Tools.
  2. Overtake Modules, then MONO2K. Bank A plays: press Play.
  3. Back parks it, playing on; Shift + Back closes it and saves. Shift + volume touch + jog click is always the way back to Move.

Then the manual: the pads first.

Updating

Close MONO2K first with Shift + Back (a parked one keeps the old version loaded), then install the new package the same way. Your patterns and sounds stay: they live outside the module, in /data/UserData/schwung/mono2k/.

Removing

Remove MONO2K in Schwung Manager. Its saves stay in /data/UserData/schwung/mono2k/; delete that folder too to remove them. Removing Schwung itself is Schwung's own job: see its site.

Tempo with Link (optional)

MONO2K always follows Move's tempo. For MONO2K's tempo knob to set Move's too, turn on Link in Move's settings and Move->Schwung in Schwung's Global Settings, Audio. It has a cost: the manual says what.

At your own risk

Schwung and MONO2K are unofficial: Ableton doesn't support them. Back up your sets before you start. MONO2K is not affiliated with Ableton, M-VAVE or Elektron.

MONO2K runs on Schwung (MIT), an open framework for Move's modules. MONO2K itself is GPL-3.0-only; its source is on GitHub.
