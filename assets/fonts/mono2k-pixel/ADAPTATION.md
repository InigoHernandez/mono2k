# MONO2K's adaptation of the Elektron Pixel Font

- **The original:** "Elektron Pixel Font", a FontStruction by savingaurora
  (https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/70152), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
  (`license.txt`, with `readme.txt`; the two travel with `mono2k-font.otf`, the owner's copy).
- **The adaptation:** MONO2K's screen type, the glyphs cropped to their ink, set one design pixel apart, some redrawn
  (the question mark within the capitals), and rendered as 1-bit bitmaps drawn at two and three times their size
  (`tools/gen_font.py` writes them to `firmware/gen/m2k_fonts.h`).

  under a later version with the same license elements. CC BY-SA 4.0 is one-way compatible with GPL-3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/licensing-considerations/compatible-licenses), the licence of the
  MONO2K firmware the bitmaps are built into.
